# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Brightwater Analytics gives every full-time employee paid time off for vacation and personal needs. PTO is separate from sick leave and company holidays.

## Accrual by Years of Service

PTO is based on completed years of service. Employees with less than 2 years of service receive 15 PTO days per year. Employees with 2 to less than 5 years of service receive 20 PTO days per year. Employees with 5 or more years of service receive 25 PTO days per year.

## First-Year Proration

PTO is prorated by the number of months worked in the calendar year. For example, an employee who starts in July works 6 months that year and receives half of the annual amount. Prorated balances are rounded down to the nearest half day.

## Carryover

You may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any unused days above 5 expire on December 31. PTO is not paid out at year end.

## Requesting PTO

Submit PTO requests in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Your manager approves or declines requests within five business days.
