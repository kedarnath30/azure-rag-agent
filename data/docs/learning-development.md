# Learning and Development

Brightwater Analytics invests in employee growth through training, conferences, and certifications.

## Annual Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000. The budget covers online courses, books, conference tickets, and certification exam fees. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Learning Days

Employees may take up to 3 paid learning days per year to attend training or study for a certification. Learning days do not count against PTO.

## Tuition Assistance

After one year of employment, employees can apply for tuition assistance of up to $5,250 per year for degree programs related to their role.
