# New Hire Onboarding

The onboarding program helps new employees get productive and connected during their first 90 days.

## First Week

On your first day you meet your manager, receive your laptop, and complete benefits enrollment in the HR portal. You are assigned an onboarding buddy from another team during your first week.

## 30-60-90 Day Plan

Your manager creates a 30-60-90 day plan with clear goals. You have check-ins with your manager at 30, 60, and 90 days.

## Probation

The first 90 days are an introductory period. At the end of it, your manager confirms your role and you become eligible for parental leave.
