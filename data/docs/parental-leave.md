# Parental Leave Policy

Brightwater Analytics supports new parents through birth, adoption, and foster placement.

## Paid Leave

The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for parental leave after 90 days of employment.

## Returning to Work

Parents may return on a reduced schedule of 30 hours per week for the first four weeks back, at full pay.
