# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses that are approved in advance.

## Submitting Expenses

Submit expense reports with itemized receipts within 30 days of the purchase. Reports older than 30 days require director approval. Reimbursements are paid with the next payroll cycle.

## Travel and Meals

The daily meal limit while traveling for business is $75. Book economy class flights for trips under six hours. Hotel stays should not exceed $250 per night unless approved by your manager.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and upgrades are not reimbursed.
