# Equipment and IT Support

Brightwater Analytics provides the equipment employees need to do their jobs.

## Laptops

Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or sooner if they fail. Do not install unapproved software on company devices.

## IT Support

For a laptop problem, password reset, or software request, contact the IT helpdesk by email at helpdesk@brightwater.example or through the IT portal. The helpdesk responds within four business hours.

## Lost or Stolen Devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours so it can be locked and wiped remotely.
