# Dental and Vision Coverage

Dental and vision plans are offered alongside medical insurance and are fully paid by the company for employees.

## Dental

The dental plan covers two cleanings per year at 100 percent. Fillings are covered at 80 percent and major work such as crowns at 50 percent, up to an annual maximum of $2,000.

## Vision

The vision plan covers one eye exam every 12 months. You can get new glasses or contact lenses every 24 months, with an allowance of $200 for frames.
