# 401(k) Retirement Plan

Brightwater Analytics offers a 401(k) retirement savings plan to help employees save for the future.

## Employer Match

The company matches 100% of your contributions up to 4% of your base salary. For example, if you contribute 4% of your salary, the company adds another 4%.

## Vesting

Your own contributions are always 100% yours. Employer matching contributions fully vest after 3 years of service.

## Enrollment

New employees are automatically enrolled at a 3% contribution rate after 30 days. You can change your rate or opt out at any time in the benefits portal.
