# Sick Leave Policy

Sick leave lets employees recover from illness, attend medical appointments, or care for a sick family member.

## Allowance

Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Sick days are available on your first day of employment and do not carry over to the next year.

## Doctor's Note

A doctor's note is required only when you are absent for more than three consecutive working days. For shorter absences, notify your manager before your start time.

## Extended Illness

If you need more than 10 sick days, contact HR to discuss short-term disability coverage, which pays 60 percent of base salary for up to 12 weeks.
