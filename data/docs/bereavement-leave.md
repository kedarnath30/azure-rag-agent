# Bereavement Leave

We understand that losing a loved one is difficult. Bereavement leave gives you paid time to grieve and handle arrangements.

## Allowance

Employees receive up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member, including a spouse, partner, child, parent, or sibling. Employees receive up to 2 paid days for the death of an extended family member, such as a grandparent, aunt, uncle, or cousin.

## How to Request

Tell your manager as soon as you are able. No documentation is required.
