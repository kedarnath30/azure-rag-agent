# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Brightwater Analytics uses a hybrid work model that balances flexibility with in-person collaboration.

## Hybrid Schedule

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are team office days for most departments.

## Home Office Stipend

Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of $500 to buy a desk, chair, or other ergonomic equipment. Submit receipts through the expense system within 90 days of your start date.

## Working From Another Location

You may work from another city for up to four weeks per year with manager approval. Working from outside the country requires HR and legal approval in advance.
