# Performance Reviews

Performance reviews help employees understand their impact and plan their growth.

## Review Cycle

Formal performance reviews happen twice a year, in April and October. Each review includes a self-assessment, manager feedback, and peer feedback from two colleagues.

## Ratings

Employees receive one of four ratings: exceeds expectations, meets expectations, partially meets expectations, or does not meet expectations.

## Compensation

Salary increases and promotions are decided during the April review cycle and take effect on May 1.
