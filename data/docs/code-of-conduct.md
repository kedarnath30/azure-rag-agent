# Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to act with integrity, respect, and professionalism.

## Respectful Workplace

Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not tolerated. Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect.

## Conflicts of Interest

Disclose any outside job, investment, or relationship that could affect your work decisions. Gifts from vendors worth more than $100 must be declined.

## Reporting Concerns

Report a code of conduct violation to your manager, to HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. The ethics hotline is available 24 hours a day. Brightwater Analytics prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.
