# Health Insurance Benefits

Brightwater Analytics offers medical coverage to all full-time employees and their dependents.

## Plans

Employees choose between a PPO plan and a high-deductible health plan (HDHP) with a health savings account. The company contributes $1,000 per year to the HSA for employees enrolled in the HDHP.

## Premiums

The company pays 90% of the monthly premium for employees and 75% of the premium for dependents.

## Enrollment

Coverage begins on the first day of the month after your start date. Open enrollment happens every November, and changes take effect on January 1. You may also change plans within 30 days of a qualifying life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.
